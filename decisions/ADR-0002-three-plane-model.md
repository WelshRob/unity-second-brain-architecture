# ADR-0002 — Three-Plane Reference Model

**Status:** Provisional  
**Date:** 24 August 2026

## Proposal

Use three conceptual planes for future comparative architecture work:

1. **Control Plane — Unity**
2. **Knowledge Plane — Brain**
3. **Execution Plane — AI + Tools**
